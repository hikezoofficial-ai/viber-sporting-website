Who we are

Viber Sporting Company is a premium golf company bringing distinctive international golf brands to India. We partner selectively with brands defined by innovation, craftsmanship, performance and purpose — building their presence through thoughtful market positioning and carefully curated distribution.

How we work

Our focus goes beyond products. We create brand value, golfer engagement and enduring market presence across India's evolving golf landscape, combining local market access, specialist golf relationships, experience-led selling and disciplined market development.
